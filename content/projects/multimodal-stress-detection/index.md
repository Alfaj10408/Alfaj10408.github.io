An end-to-end digital-biomarker pipeline for detecting physiological stress from wearable sensor data. Heart
rate variability (HRV) and electrodermal activity (EDA) features are fused and temporal stress dynamics are
modeled with recurrent networks.

**Stack:** Python, HRV/EDA signal processing, LSTM networks.
