An experimental pipeline that converts natural-language prompts into explicit 3D meshes. Diffusion-based 3D
priors (Shap-E) are combined with NeRF-style implicit representations for view-consistent geometry synthesis.

**Stack:** Python, Shap-E, NeRF, diffusion models.
